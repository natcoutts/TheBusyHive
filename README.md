# TheBusyHive
Capturing house tasks that need doing, who does them, how difficult they are to do and who is in the running for "Task Master" this week

I asked Gemini to help me write an app to track household chores that need doing and who is doing more chores? There are four of us. I want us to be able to rate the chore as easy, medium or hard and each completed chore earns points (1 for easy chores, 2 for medium chores, 3 for hard chores). At the end of the week the one that has gained the most points can assign chores to those that have fewer points.
